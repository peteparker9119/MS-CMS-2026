Convergence Management System

The single platform for inter-unit convergence across Tamil Nadu's 7 school-support units and 21 unit pairs — meetings, minutes and action points, all in one place.

📅

Plan meetings. Schedule across all 21 unit pairs with agendas and auto-notifications.

📋

Record minutes. File MoMs and capture action points right after each meeting.

✅

Track action items. Pending items surface on your dashboard until closed.

📊

Convergence matrix. 7 × 7 health grid shows pair-wise progress at a glance.
